PILOT DEMONSTRATION

Nagaon, Assam

Nagaon is used only as the initial demonstration area. FLOODSAFE is designed to be adapted to other flood-prone regions by replacing the verified local GIS layers and flood inventory.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT?

Prediction → explanation → action

Instead of ending with a research map, FLOODSAFE presents the result as location information that a community member can understand.

WHAT WE WILL MEASURE

Evidence before final claims

Accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score and ROC-AUC will be calculated only after training and validating the verified project dataset.

SCALABLE BY DESIGN

One platform. Multiple flood-prone regions.

The interface is not tied to one district. A deployment can switch the region, flood inventory, rainfall period and GIS layers while keeping the same RFE → GBM → GIS → community workflow.

METHODOLOGY

RFE + Gradient Boosting Machine

The selected methodology from Review 1 is shown step-by-step instead of as one confusing box.

1

Prepare data

Align all predictor layers to the same CRS, extent and 30 m grid. Handle NoData and extract samples.

2

RFE

Recursive Feature Elimination ranks features and removes less useful variables to create a compact feature set.

3

Train GBM

Gradient Boosting combines decision trees sequentially to learn nonlinear relationships between factors and flood labels.

4

Validate

Evaluate predictions using accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score and ROC-AUC.

5

Map output

Predict a probability for each pixel and classify the continuous surface into susceptibility zones.

Why this methodology?

It connects feature selection with a nonlinear ML model

RFE helps reduce the input feature set. GBM then learns nonlinear patterns from the selected factors. The resulting probability can be visualised spatially in GIS.

DATASET

What goes into FLOODSAFE?

The production dataset is a pixel/sample table created by combining a verified flood inventory with 13 geospatial conditioning factors.

Flood label

1 = flood, 0 = non-flood. The published Nagaon study built its flood inventory for 2008–2023 using Bhuvan/ISRO, Sentinel-1 SAR and verification/digitisation.

Spatial standardisation

The published workflow standardised the conditioning factors to 30 × 30 m and matched CRS, extent and pixel size before extraction.

Data split

The published Nagaon study used 80% training and 20% validation. FLOODSAFE should recompute its own split and metrics on its verified dataset.

COMMUNITY MODULE

What does the community get?

The model output is translated into simple information instead of showing only technical GIS layers.

🟢 Low / Moderate

Keep monitoring rainfall and official advisories. Prepare essential documents, medicines and emergency contacts.

🟠 High

Prepare for possible waterlogging/flooding. Charge phones, secure valuables and identify a safer higher location.

🔴 Very High

Treat as a high-awareness zone. Follow official evacuation or emergency instructions and avoid moving floodwater.

Before flood

  • Keep emergency items ready.
  • Know safe routes.
  • Monitor official alerts.

During flood

  • Do not drive/walk through moving water.
  • Avoid electrical hazards.
  • Follow local instructions.

After flood

  • Avoid contaminated water.
  • Report hazards.
  • Return after official clearance.
